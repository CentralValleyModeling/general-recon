#
##### Table Notes  
**NESP**: Non-Exceedance Subsidence Profile  
**CAA**: California Aqueduct  
**DCP**: Delta Conveyance Project  
**FIRO**: Forecast Informed Reservoir Operations  
**SODS**: South of Delta Storage  
**LOC**: Level of Concern; a model ensemble informed quasi-exceedance probability that system performance would be better than *n* percent of the system performance outcomes under alternative model-informed conditions.  
**TUCP**: Temporary Urgency Change Petition; a request to the SWRCB to temporarily change the terms and conditions of an existing water right, permit, or license.  
**LTO**: 2024 Final EIR for the Long-Term Operation of the State Water Project in the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta, Suisun Marsh, and Suisun Bay
